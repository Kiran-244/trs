The Teacher
Who Walked In

January 2024

The next day, she walked into the classroom.

Ms. Binderpal Kaur.

Before she even spoke, something about her felt strangely familiar.

"I've seen her somewhere before..."

For a moment, I couldn't remember where.

Then it came back to me.

I had seen her around the hostel a few times.

Nothing extraordinary had happened.

We had never spoken.

It was simply one of those ordinary moments that somehow chooses to stay with you.

Since our English lecture was combined with the Mathematics Department, the classroom was unusually full.

She looked around, smiled warmly, and said,

"Such a big class."

A few students smiled.

The room instantly felt lighter.

Then she did something I hadn't expected.

Instead of beginning with the syllabus...

she began with us.

One by one.

Seat by seat.

Every student introduced themselves.

She listened patiently to each person, never rushing anyone.

And while the introductions continued, I found myself noticing something else.

The confidence.

The clarity.

The effortless fluency with which she spoke.

It wasn't just her English.

It was the ease with which she expressed every thought—with confidence, grace, and simplicity.

I remember thinking,

"I wish I could speak like that someday."

That was the first lesson I learned from her.

She hadn't taught a single chapter yet,
but she had already become someone I wanted to learn from.
