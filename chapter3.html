January 2024

Slowly, your classroom began to feel different from every other classroom I attended.

In many lectures, learning often felt one-sided.

Teachers explained.

Students wrote.

The lecture ended.

That was usually where the conversation stopped.

But your classroom wasn't like that.

You asked questions.

You encouraged discussions.

You welcomed different opinions.

You gave students the freedom to think before answering.

Even when someone gave the wrong answer, you never made them feel embarrassed.

Instead, you turned mistakes into conversations.

That fascinated me.

I have always loved asking questions.

I have always loved speaking my mind.

I have always loved understanding why something is true rather than simply accepting it.

Perhaps that's why your classes slowly became my favourite.

They never felt like lectures.

They felt like conversations.

And somewhere between those conversations, I realized something.

"You weren't just teaching us English.
You were teaching us how to think."

Some teachers fill notebooks with notes.

The best ones fill minds with questions.
