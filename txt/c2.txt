Chapter 2
The First Hello

The next day, she walked into the classroom.

Ms. Binderpal Kaur.

Before she even spoke, something felt familiar.

"I've seen her somewhere..."

And then it struck me.

I had seen her around the hostel before.

It wasn't a significant moment.

Just a passing thought.

But somehow, I still remember it.

Since our lecture was combined with the Mathematics Department, the classroom was unusually full.

Looking around, she smiled and said,

"Such a big class."

Then she did something I didn't expect.

Instead of beginning with the syllabus, she began with us.

One by one.

Every student introduced themselves.

Seat by seat.

She listened patiently to everyone.

And while she spoke, I found myself noticing something.

The confidence.

The clarity.

The effortless fluency with which she communicated.

I remember thinking,

"I wish I could speak like that someday."
