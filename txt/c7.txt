Chapter 7
Questions I'll Never Forget

Some teachers teach lessons.

You asked questions.

Questions that stayed long after the lecture ended.

One day you asked,

"What is God?"

Everyone answered differently.

When my turn came, I said,

"God is hope. God is faith."

Ironically, we had just received our first-semester results that day.

I was grateful for the SGPA I had earned.

Perhaps that gratitude shaped my answer.

But then you shared a personal story about losing a family member.

You spoke about grief.

About hope.

About the difficult things we tell ourselves to keep moving forward.

That conversation wasn't part of the syllabus.

Yet it became one of the lessons I remember most.

It reminded me that education isn't only about grammar or literature.

Sometimes, it's about understanding life itself.
