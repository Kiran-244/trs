Chapter 5
The Day I Discovered Something About Myself

Then came the Poetry Competition.

February 2, 2024.

Strange how some dates stay with us forever.

My friends were participating.

So I thought...

Maybe I should try too.

The funny part?

I had never written poetry before.

Not once.

Throughout school, I had always been the studious child.

The one who focused on marks.

Writing creatively had never felt like something I could do.

Yet there I was...

Registering for an English poetry competition.

English.

The language in which expressing emotions felt even harder.

I still remember writing my poem on a rough sheet first.

My roommate listened to it and gave me honest feedback.

I rewrote parts of it.

Then submitted the final version.

Before handing it in, I quietly slipped the rough draft into my bag.

I wanted to keep it.

It was the first poem I had ever written.

A small beginning.

Or so I thought.

When the results were announced...

I stood there in disbelief.

First Position.

I couldn't believe it.

That day taught me something I still carry with me.

We lose countless opportunities—not because we fail, but because we never give ourselves the chance to try.

Who knows?

Maybe I could have been a swimmer.

Maybe a martial artist.

Maybe something entirely different.

But I would never know unless I dared to begin.

Sometimes...

Trying is the greatest victory.
