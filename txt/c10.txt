There are many teachers I have learned from.

Many classrooms I have sat in.

Many lectures that began with attendance and ended with the ringing of a bell.

With time, most of them quietly became memories.

But very few stayed with me long after the lecture ended.

You did.

For a long time, I wondered why.

Was it the subject you taught?

The stories you shared?

The questions you asked?

Or the way your classroom always felt different?

Perhaps it wasn't one extraordinary moment.

Perhaps it was a hundred ordinary moments, stitched together so gently that I didn't even realize they were changing me.

And maybe that's what makes a truly remarkable teacher.

They don't just leave you with notes.

They leave you with a different way of seeing the world.

Years from now, I may forget what we studied, the grammar rules we revised, or the assignments I submitted.

But I don't think I'll ever forget the way your classroom made me feel.

Curious.

Confident.

Heard.

Inspired.

Thank you for creating a classroom where learning never felt limited to the syllabus.

Thank you for asking questions that made me think long after the lecture ended.

Thank you for sharing your stories so honestly, because they reminded me that teachers are people too—with their own childhood memories, struggles, and lessons.

Thank you for encouraging conversations instead of simply delivering lectures.

Thank you for proving that kindness can be one of the strongest forms of leadership.

There are many things I admire about you—your confidence, your clarity, your warmth, your humility—but above all, I admire the kind of person you choose to be every single day.

I hope you never stop asking thought-provoking questions.

I hope you never stop sharing little stories from your life.

I hope you never stop creating a classroom where students feel comfortable enough to think aloud, to make mistakes, and to discover themselves.

Because somewhere, perhaps without even realizing it, you are helping someone believe in themselves a little more.

You certainly did that for me.

Sometimes, I wonder whether teachers ever realize how deeply they influence their students.

Maybe they don't.

Maybe that's why I wanted to create this little diary.

So that, just once, you could see yourself through the eyes of one of your students.

Not as an English teacher.

But as someone who quietly inspired confidence, curiosity, and a love for learning.

Happy Teachers' Day, Ma'am.

Thank you for every lesson, every conversation, every story, and every moment that made my university life a little richer.

I hope you continue touching lives exactly the way you have touched mine.

With heartfelt gratitude and immense respect,

Kiranpreet Kaur
