A Small Moment That Stayed

My turn to introduce myself came a day later.

It was such a tiny moment.

Yet somehow, it stayed with me.

I stood up.

My bag was still resting awkwardly on my lap.

As I got up, half of it slipped down.

For a split second, I wondered...

Should I adjust it?

Or should I just continue?

I decided to continue.

I introduced myself.

Sat back down.

And spent the rest of the lecture replaying that tiny awkward moment in my head.

You probably don't remember it.

Honestly...

I don't expect you to.

But I do.

Funny how life works.

The moments that seem insignificant to one person sometimes become unforgettable to another.
