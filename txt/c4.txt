Chapter 4
A Classroom That Felt Different

Slowly, your classroom became different from every other classroom I attended.

In many lectures, learning often felt one-sided.

Teachers explained.

Students wrote.

The lecture ended.

But your classroom wasn't like that.

You asked questions.

You encouraged discussions.

You welcomed different opinions.

You gave students the freedom to think before answering.

Even when someone was wrong, you never made them feel embarrassed.

Instead, you turned mistakes into conversations.

That fascinated me.

I have always loved speaking.

I have always loved questioning.

I have always loved discovering why something is true rather than simply accepting it.

And perhaps that is why your classes slowly became my favourite.

They didn't just teach English.

They taught curiosity.
