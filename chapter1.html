January 2024

The second semester of my first year at Akal University had just begun.

It was our very first English Reading and Writing lecture of the semester. Like everyone else, I waited for our English teacher to walk into the classroom.

A few moments later, a teacher entered. She introduced the course, explained the syllabus with remarkable clarity, and gave us what she called a bird's-eye view of everything we would be learning throughout the semester.

Listening to her, I naturally assumed she was our English teacher.

As the lecture came to an end, she smiled and said,

"Your English teacher is absent today. She'll be taking your class from tomorrow."

For a brief moment, I was surprised.

So, she wasn't our English teacher after all.

Her name was Ms. Amanjeet Brar, and she had taken our class as an adjustment lecture.

As I walked out of the classroom, I remember thinking,

"I wish she were our English teacher."

Little did I know that tomorrow would introduce me to someone who would leave a far deeper impression on my university journey.
