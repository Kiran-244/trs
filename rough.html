January 2024

The next day, it was my turn to introduce myself.

It was such a small moment.

Almost forgettable.

Yet somehow, it stayed with me.

I stood up.

My bag was still resting awkwardly on my lap.

As I got to my feet, half of it slipped down.

For a split second, I hesitated.

"Should I adjust it...
or should I just continue?"

I chose to continue.

I introduced myself.

Sat back down.

And spent the rest of the lecture replaying that tiny awkward moment in my head.

You probably don't remember it.

Honestly...

I don't expect you to.

But I do.

Funny how memory works.

It quietly holds on to moments that everyone else has forgotten.

The smallest moments often become the ones we carry the longest.

Perhaps that's what teachers never get to see.

The little moments they forget...
sometimes become memories their students never do.
