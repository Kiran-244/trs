I’m writing this today, after you have already read it, in case you’ve come back to it, just as I do almost every week, sometimes even twice or thrice a week. I thought I should write this little note. Although you are under no obligation whatsoever, you may read it if you wish.

YOU MIGHT WONDER WHY UNDER THE HIGHEST HEAVEN WOULD SHE EVER WANT TO WRITE SOMETHING LIKE THIS?

But lemme tell you, this wasn't planned at all. The idea came to me in a flash, striking me just as I began reflecting on these three years of university life, especially when I realised that I was about to enter the fourth and final year of my graduation. And, as humans, we do tend to make sense of things in retrospect, I guess.

Then all the sweet and memorable events began coming back to me, one after another (the salty ones, too... they somehow found their way in). And it suddenly occurred to me that ERW classes used to be fun! and that was because of YOUU Like, literally so much fun and genuinely riveting, at least for me personally. It made me wonder: what if I could create something that captured all those sweet memories about you as a person and as a teacher, something I could return to whenever I felt like revisiting those good old days?

I started with a rough idea.I thought about the entire process of what could be done to make a keepsake and it was all taking shape in my head while I was trying to fall asleep one night in july.

And I've always loved crafts, creativity, and books. So, at the intersection of all these things sat the idea of creating a beautiful digital keepsake, perhaps in the form of a website (that's the only thing I know in the name of B.Tech... haha, I'm saying that tongue-in-cheek ;)) where I could bring all these ideas together and create something for myself.

"Since it was about youu ❤️ and your classes, why should it not find it's way to you is what occured to me while designing it."

And so, I thought I'd send it to you, because life is too short to hold back our affection and feelings for people we admire.

I genuinely loved the entire process of creating this. It was more of a mini passion project for me. Once the idea had taken shape, all I had to do was move into the execution phase. So, the next day, I wrote a script, divided it into chapters, decided on titles for each one, and put the events in a sequence, because they weren't arranged chronologically in my mind. Then I drafted each chapter before finalising every single word. After all, you are an English teacher, and I make quite a lot of mistakes while typing and framing sentences. Keeping that little constraint in mind, I thought I had to come up with something simple yet structured, something that read more like a story than an essay about Ms. Binderpal Kaur and her thought-provoking classes. :) By the way, writing this little note isn't such a bad idea either.

I opine everything that we choose to do shapes us in some way or the other. And this little labor of love helped me discover something that wasn't entirely new but deeply meaningful.

It made me realise that when we do something of our own accord, without being asked or expected to do it, we tend to lose ourselves in the process. That is what people call mindfulness, or a flow state, when we lose track of time because we're so immersed in what we're doing. And somehow, the act itself begins to feel sacred—not in a religious sense, perhaps, but figuratively, in its own quiet way.

't was rather ironic to me because I don't enjoy working on the projects prescribed in our curriculum nearly as much as I enjoy experimenting with things my heart genuinely loves to do, enjoy, and savour.

Now that the website was ready, the next step was to decide whether I should keep it to myself, tucked away in my system, where I alone could revisit it and cherish those moments, or send it to you. I hesitated a little before sharing it with you (and yes... this is between us, because I haven't shared it with anyone else. Like, nobody!), simply because I didn't want to come across as someone who was being over-the-top affectionate. Sending something like this out of the blue might have seemed a little awkward. I tend to think thrice before messaging a teacher, out of respect for boundaries and for the fact that you have a busy schedule of your own. But then, since you're a literature person, you'd probably know better than I do that:

"What's done out of love always takes place beyond good and evil"

Then Guru Purnima came and that felt like a perfect occasion to send this not for you to praise this effort but because It was meant for you and had to reach to you some day or the other...

I don't really know how this might feel from your end, and perhaps it is a little unusual to receive something like this out of the blue. But I hope it brings a smile to your face, just as the memories behind it bring one to mine :)
