EPILOGUE

The Teacher Who Stayed

Every story eventually reaches its final page.
Before this one ends...
there is one last thing I'd like to say.

❤️ 
Dear Ma'am,

This memoir isn't really about English.

It's about a teacher who unknowingly made a lasting place for herself in the memories of one of her students.

If someone were to ask me years from now about my university life, I know I wouldn't begin by talking about grades, assignments, or examinations.

I would begin with people.

Because that's what we remember in the end.

And somewhere in those memories...

there would always be an English teacher named Ms. Binderpal Kaur.

"The best teachers don't ask to be remembered.
They simply live in the stories their students continue to tell."

Maybe that's why I wanted to write this memoir.

So that, for just a little while,
you could see yourself through the eyes of one of your students.

You once taught us that stories matter.

This one is yours.



I hope you never stop being the person you are.
I hope you continue sharing your stories, asking thought-provoking questions, and creating classrooms where students feel comfortable enough to think, to speak, and to grow.
But I hope this memoir reminds you that your kindness, your warmth, and your passion for teaching leave an impression that lasts far beyond the classroom.
You are not only an amazing teacher.
You are an amazing person.
Please keep passing that kindness, curiosity, and love for learning to every student who walks into your classroom.
And students like me are incredibly fortunate to have learned from one.








Once again,





Happy Teachers' Day, Ma'am.



More than anything,

thank you for being

The Teacher Who Stayed.

With heartfelt gratitude,

Kiranpreet Kaur
